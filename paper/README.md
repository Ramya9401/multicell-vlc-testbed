# Paper

## What you may put in this directory

The paper is published by IEEE. If the standard Copyright Transfer Form was
signed, what you can post is restricted:

| Version | May it go in this directory? |
|---|---|
| **Accepted manuscript** (post-review, before IEEE typesetting) | **Yes** — with the notice below |
| **Preprint** (as submitted) | **Yes** |
| **Published PDF** (IEEE-formatted, Xplore header/footer) | **No**, unless the paper is IEEE Open Access / CC-BY |
| Title, abstract, DOI link, BibTeX | Yes, always |
| Your own figures and results | Yes — add the IEEE credit line if reproduced exactly from the published article |

**Check the Xplore page for a CC-BY badge first.** If the paper is open
access, the published PDF can go here as-is.

Suggested filename: `accepted_manuscript.pdf`

## Required notice

Any accepted-manuscript PDF posted here must carry, on its first page:
